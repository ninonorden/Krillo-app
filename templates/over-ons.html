Built for store owners first

More and more people ask ChatGPT or Gemini for a recommendation instead of searching themselves. For a store that means something simple and uncomfortable at once: if the assistant does not know your shop, you do not exist for that buyer. And almost no store owner currently knows whether that is happening.

Tools for this exist, but nearly all of them are built for marketing agencies or large companies, with the prices and dashboards that go with that. Krillo is built for the other end of the market: the owner who runs their own store, has no agency, and simply wants to know what is going on and what to do about it.

How we work

No jargon, and no dashboard that needs a manual. You see what is wrong in plain words, and we write the solution: texts written for your products, and the technical part underneath. Let us do it, and we put it in your store ourselves.

Checking your store and reading the rankings stays free, with no account and no payment details. You pay when you want your rank and the fixes every month (Watch, EUR 49 a month) or want us to make the changes in your store (Fix, EUR 149 a month).
