Withdraw your purchase

Want to undo your purchase? Fill in this form. You do not have to give a reason.

Good to know If you cancel within fourteen days of your first payment, you get that first month back, whether you are a consumer or a business. As a consumer you also have a legal cooling-off period of fourteen days. For work we do in your store (Fix), that right ends once the work is fully delivered, and only if you explicitly agreed to that when ordering. If you withdraw while we are halfway, you only pay for the part we already did. Not sure whether this applies to you? Just fill in the form, we will look at it and let you know.
