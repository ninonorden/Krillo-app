What is AI visibility exactly?

AI visibility is the question of whether assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity can find, understand and recommend your store. More and more buyers ask an assistant for a recommendation instead of searching themselves. If the assistant does not know your store, you miss that customer without noticing.

Is this the same as SEO?

No. Ordinary search engine optimisation is about ranking high in a list of results. With AI it is about whether your store is named in the answer itself. That depends on different things, such as whether AI can read your product information and whether your site does not accidentally shut AI crawlers out.

Is the check really free?

Yes. You enter your store address and see your score and every finding within about a minute, with no account and no payment details. You pay only when you want more: Watch, where you get your rank every month, or Fix, where we install the solutions in your store.

What does Fix mean?

We make the improvements in your store ourselves, you paste nothing. For every finding we write the solution, aimed at your products and your sector. On Shopify our app writes them into your store: image descriptions, product descriptions that are too short, and a page with questions and answers. On other platforms our team makes the changes by hand, once you have given us access. Every change is kept with the old text beside it, and anything you dislike we roll back.

Do I need to be technical to use this?

No. Everything is explained in plain words, without jargon. For technical changes you get the code ready to use, with where to put it.

How does the subscription work?

Every month we put thirty buying questions to ChatGPT and Gemini, questions buyers in your category really ask, and see whether your store is in the answer and whether you are named or actually recommended. Every mention comes with the quote from the answer. From that measurement we take at most three things that gain the most, and with Fix we make those changes in your store. You get a message of what changed, with the old text, so you can always roll it back. If you would rather not give us access, you get those same three things ready to do yourself: the questions and answers written about your products, with a copy button and the route through your own admin screen. If there is a page on the internet that lists a competitor and not you, you get that link and the email to its editors. All those numbers sit behind a link on your own page. You can cancel monthly, there is no minimum term.

Is this not just an SEO check with AI sauce on it?

Fair question, because half of what we do is indeed technical work you can find elsewhere: does your robots.txt let AI crawlers in, is your text in the page itself, is your product information machine readable. Those are thirteen checks and we are not unique in them. If your store runs on professional shop software, part of it is probably fine already. What matters is the other half: we put real buying questions to ChatGPT and Gemini and see whether your store is in the answer. That is not a check on your site, it is a measurement of the outcome. And it is the only way to know whether all that technical work is getting you anything.

Does Google not already show AI results for free in Search Console?

It does. Since June 2026 there is a report on generative AI in Search Console, and it is free. Use it, it is useful. But it measures something different. Search Console only covers Google's own AI surfaces, so AI Overviews and AI Mode. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude are not in it, and a large part of buying questions is asked there. It also only gives impressions: how often a page of yours appeared in such an answer. You do not see which question was asked, you do not see which other stores were in that same answer, and you do not see whether you were named or actually recommended. That last one is the difference between "you could also look at X" and "the raincoats from X last longest", and only the second gets you a customer. We ask the buying questions ourselves, to ChatGPT and Gemini, and show per question what answered and on what grounds.
