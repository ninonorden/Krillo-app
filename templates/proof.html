Does being named by AI sell?

Not by itself. A store can be named in a hundred answers and sell nothing from it. So the right question is not "are we named?", but "does being named bring visits, orders and revenue?" This page shows how to check that, in your own numbers.

The chain

Named by AI → a visit from AI → engaged visitor → order → revenue. Every step needs its own number:

  • Named: in how many buying questions AI names or recommends you. Krillo measures this every month, and your key questions every week.
  • Visits from AI: sessions that come from chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, perplexity.ai and copilot.microsoft.com.
  • Engagement: pages per visit and time on site of those visits, compared with your other traffic.
  • Orders: conversions and conversion rate of AI visits.
  • Revenue: revenue from AI visits, and orders where AI was one of the steps before the purchase (assisted conversions).

Where you see it

Google Analytics 4: Reports, Acquisition, Traffic acquisition. Set the table to "Session source" and filter on chatgpt, gemini, perplexity and copilot. Add the columns Sessions, Key events and Total revenue. For the steps before a purchase: Advertising, Attribution paths.

Shopify: Analytics, Reports, "Sessions by referrer" and "Sales by referrer". AI assistants show up under their own domain.

In your Krillo dashboard: visits from AI, orders and revenue, every month. Krillo puts them next to your AI rank for the same month, with your first month as the baseline, so you see the whole chain on one screen. You connect it once with the Krillo pixel (two minutes on Shopify, a few on WooCommerce) and the numbers come in by themselves. You can also add a month by hand.

Note: not every AI visit is labelled. Apps and some browsers send no source, and those visits land under "direct". The real number is usually higher than what you see.

How to show it is cause, not chance

  1. Baseline: write down four weeks of AI visits, orders and revenue before you change anything, and your Krillo numbers for the same weeks.
  2. Change one thing: the fix Krillo suggests for one group of questions or one category page. Note the date.
  3. Measure after: four weeks of the same numbers. Krillo asks your five key questions again every week and all buying questions every month, so you see when you start being named.
  4. Compare with a control: a category or a set of pages you did not change, over the same weeks. If only the changed part moves, that is your evidence.

The return, in one line

Every month your dashboard compares your revenue from AI with your baseline month, and puts it next to what Krillo costs you. For example: “€204 more revenue from AI in September than in your baseline month. Krillo costs you €49 a month: that is 4.2x what you pay.” Your own numbers, so you can check every euro. If there is no extra revenue yet, it says so.

The honest limits

Seasons and sales move your numbers too. AI models are updated and answers change without you doing anything. With few visits, chance plays a big role, so look at several weeks, not one. And attribution is never complete, because part of the AI traffic arrives without a label. That is why we show the trend over time and a comparison with what you did not change, never one number as proof.
