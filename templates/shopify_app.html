Questions people ask us

Short answers, no small print.

Does this change anything in my store?
Only when you press the button, and only what you selected. We show you every proposed change first, in full, and nothing happens until you agree. We fill in three things: descriptions on images that have none, product descriptions that are missing or very short (under 180 characters), and a page with frequently asked questions if you do not already have one. We keep the old text of every change, and if you change a product between our proposal and your click, we skip that item. With Fix we also do this every week without asking first; you get an email listing every change and you can switch it off. We never touch your prices, stock, orders, customer data or theme.
Can I undo what you changed?
Yes, every single change, one at a time. Before we write anything we store what was there before. If the old value cannot be stored, we do not write: a change that cannot be undone is worse than no change. Under "What we changed in your store" there is an Undo button next to each one.
Why do you need permission to edit products and pages?
Because we do the work instead of handing you a to-do list. To write a description into a product or add a page, Shopify needs us to have that permission. We also need access to your files, because the description on an image is stored with the file and not with the product. We ask for nothing beyond products, pages and images: no customers, no orders, no checkout.
How can you know what ChatGPT says?
We ask it. Krillo sends real shopping questions to ChatGPT and Gemini and reads the answers. For every question we keep the quote the verdict is based on, so you can check it yourself. We do not ask a model why it recommends a store, because that answer is made up after the fact and tells you nothing.
Will this get me mentioned by ChatGPT?
We cannot promise that, and you should be careful with anyone who does. Most of what an AI says about a store comes from other people's pages: comparison articles, forums, review sites. What we can do is measure whether you are mentioned today, make sure nothing on your own site is blocking or confusing AI, and show you the pages your competitors appear on and you don't.
Why is my rank not there yet?
We place a new store in its category overnight and work out its rank from the answers of the last measurement of that category. So you usually see it the day after installing. If we have not measured your category yet, it waits until it is its turn; that can take a few days.
Which language and country do you measure in?
The Krillo index measures stores selling in the Netherlands and Belgium, with questions in the language shoppers there use. More countries follow. Your store's market comes from Shopify when you install Krillo. {% if markt %}Yours is set to {{ markt.taal }} ({{ markt.landcode }}).{% endif %}
What data do you store?
The public content of your store, the questions we generated, the answers we got and your store's email address so we can reach you. We never store anything about your customers, and we never see their orders. Remove the app and your access key is deleted straight away. Shopify then asks us to erase the rest 48 hours later, and we do.
Krillo asks the questions for real and reads the answers. We count per question rather than per answer, and we show counts rather than percentages, because at these numbers a percentage suggests a precision that isn't there.
