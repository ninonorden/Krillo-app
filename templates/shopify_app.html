Questions people ask us

Does this change anything in my store?
No. Krillo only reads. We look at your product pages and your settings to work out what you sell and whether AI can read it. We never touch your prices, stock, orders, customer data or theme. If we ever add the option to apply a fix for you, it will be a separate button that asks you first, and every change will be reversible.
How can you know what ChatGPT says?
We ask it. Krillo sends real shopping questions to ChatGPT and Gemini and reads the answers. For every question we keep the quote the verdict is based on, so you can check it yourself. We do not ask a model why it recommends a store, because that answer is made up after the fact and tells you nothing.
Will this get me mentioned by ChatGPT?
We cannot promise that, and you should be careful with anyone who does. Most of what an AI says about a store comes from other people's pages: comparison articles, forums, review sites. What we can do is measure whether you are mentioned today, make sure nothing on your own site is blocking or confusing AI, and show you the pages your competitors appear on and you don't.
Why does the first measurement take a few minutes?
Because we really ask the questions, one by one, to more than one model, and then read every answer. That is slower than reading a sitemap, and it is the reason the result means something. You can close this screen while it runs.
Which language and country do you measure in?
The ones your store is set to. Shopify tells us both when you install Krillo, so a store selling in the US gets questions an American shopper would type, searched in US results. {% if markt %}Yours is set to {{ markt.taal }} ({{ markt.landcode }}).{% endif %}
What data do you store?
The public content of your store, the questions we generated, the answers we got and your store's email address so we can reach you. We never store anything about your customers, and we never see their orders. Remove the app and we erase everything we hold about your store.
Krillo asks the questions for real and reads the answers. We count per question rather than per answer, and we show counts rather than percentages, because at these numbers a percentage suggests a precision that isn't there.
