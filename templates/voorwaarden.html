2. What Krillo does

Krillo measures whether AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini name and recommend online stores when shoppers ask them buying questions. We publish the outcome per category and country in a public index. On top of that we offer:

  • A free check of your store on thirteen technical points, without an account.
  • Watch, 49 euro per month: your rank every month in your category and country, the buying questions you do not appear in with the real answer, which competitor was named instead of you, the thirteen checks on your store, and the fixes written out for you to do yourself.
  • Fix, 149 euro per month: everything in Watch, and we install the fixes in your store ourselves (see article 7). At the next monthly measurement after a delivery we show you the difference, and you get a message when you drop three places or more in the ranking.
  • Brands and agencies, 490 euro per month: up to 25 stores, each with its own dashboard. We set this up together with you and confirm the details in writing.

A plan covers the country of your store. AI gives different answers per country, so another country is a separate ranking; if you sell in more than one country, email us and we look at it with you before anything is charged.

3. The public index

  • Nobody can buy a place in the index. The order follows from the answers of the AI assistants and nothing else. Being a customer does not change your place.
  • Every ranking carries the date it was measured. AI answers change, so a ranking describes that moment and not a lasting truth.
  • Do you run a store in the index and think something about it is wrong, email us. We check it against the answers we stored.

4. The free check

The free check costs nothing. Free tests with AI assistants are limited per day, because every test costs us money. If the same website is tested again within thirty days, you may see the stored result instead of a new measurement. We may change these limits.

7. When we work in your store (Fix)

  • With Fix we install the improvements in your store ourselves. For that you give us access to the admin of your store. That access is voluntary and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • We only change: texts, product descriptions, frequently asked questions, image descriptions, and settings that decide whether AI can read your site.
  • We never change: your prices, your stock, your orders, your customer data and your design.
  • We keep the old text of every change. On your dashboard you see per change what is there now and what was there before, so everything can be put back. If you are not happy with a change and tell us within thirty days, we roll it back.
  • In our Shopify app we work automatically: we fill in empty places, such as a missing image description, we replace product descriptions that are very short (under 180 characters), and we can add a page with frequently asked questions. You can undo every change there, and switch the automatic work off if you would rather approve every change yourself. Tell us the same by email and we will work that way in any store.
  • We start as soon as we have access. Count on two to five working days after that. If it takes longer, we let you know.
  • If the admin you give us access to also shows data of your customers, we process that data only as far as it is unavoidable for the work. We do not look at it, we do not copy anything from it and we do not use it for anything else. If you want a data processing agreement for this, email us and we will send one.

That is an approximation of what a user sees in the app and not a copy of it, because there the search results of that moment, location and history also play a part. Whether you are named also depends largely on sources outside your own website, which neither you nor we control.
